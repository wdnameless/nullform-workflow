# Why

Nullform should be a small native-first workflow addition people can install, understand and remove safely. Current audit proves six safety and recovery defects despite a green broad suite. Runtime internals share a flat directory, setup does not discover candidate clients in its public first-run path, and public claims must distinguish deterministic guardrails from unproven AI-quality benefit.

# What changes

- Close all six audited defects with consumer-visible regressions.
- Make one-command npm/npx setup discover and explain the selected host without guessing through ambiguity or altering human-owned settings.
- Group internal modules by responsibility, keep real executable entrypoints stable, and migrate every caller and deployment inventory.
- Reduce our managed instruction overhead and reuse native tasks/delegation/tools instead of adding parallel systems.
- Present reproducible compatibility and guardrail evidence, a contributor navigation path and an honest exact-artifact release process.

# What does not change

No paid experiments, automatic model routing/fallback, new database/server/framework, cloned task board, identity replacement, live personal configuration edits, or claimed universal superiority. Current historical evidence remains unchanged. Publication and release acceptance require their real prerequisites, not fixture substitutes.
